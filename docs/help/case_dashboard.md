# Case Dashboard Help

## Purpose

The Case Dashboard is used to review linked alerts, evidence, risk scoring, findings, recommendations, narrative, and exports.

## Recommended Workflow

1. Open the case.
2. Generate Case Summary.
3. Review risk score.
4. Review findings and recommendations.
5. Generate Investigation Narrative.
6. Export narrative to notes.
7. Export full case bundle.
8. Verify bundle integrity.

## What to Check

- Risk score matches alert severity.
- Narrative does not contradict risk score.
- Evidence mappings are present.
- Chain of custody timeline exists.
- Evidence hashes are present in bundle.
